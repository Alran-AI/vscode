# Connect a model

**Sign in with Ottili.** Your browser opens the Ottili sign-in page. In a remote window (SSH, WSL, containers, Codespaces) Alran shows a short code instead, which you confirm in any browser. New accounts get welcome credit for Ottili Werk.

**Ottili API key.** For machines without a browser: paste a key from [ai.ottili.one/api-keys](https://ai.ottili.one/api-keys).

**Your own provider.** Any OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic or Gemini endpoint with your own key. Those calls go straight to the provider; Ottili does not see or bill them.

Your account then appears in the editor's **Accounts** menu and in the **Account** view of the Alran sidebar, with company, project, credit and spending.
